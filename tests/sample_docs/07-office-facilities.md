# Office facilities

## Parking
Employees may park in garage B for 40 USD per month. Visitors park free in garage A for up to 2 hours.

## Visitors
Visitors must register at reception, wear a badge and be escorted at all times.

## Pets
Dogs are allowed in the office on Thursdays with prior notice to facilities.

## Cafeteria
The cafeteria is open from 8:00 to 15:00. Vegetarian and vegan options are available every day.
