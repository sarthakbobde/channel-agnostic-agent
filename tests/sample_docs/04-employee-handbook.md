# Employee handbook

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Employees may start between 8:00 and 10:00. Lunch is 45 minutes and is not paid. Overtime must be approved by the manager in advance.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. A one-time home-office allowance of 400 USD is paid after the probation period.

## Probation
The probation period is 90 days. Notice during probation is 7 days. After probation the notice period is 60 days.

## Training
Every employee has a yearly training budget of 1500 USD. Courses must be approved by the manager before booking.
