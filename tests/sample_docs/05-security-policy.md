# Security policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 180 days. Never share a password, even with the IT team. Multi-factor authentication is required for the VPN and email.

## Phishing
Report phishing emails to security-desk@example.com within one hour. Do not click links in unexpected emails. The security team runs a phishing test every quarter.

## Data classification
Documents are Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted documents may only be shared with named people and never by personal email.
